## Notes

- **AirPods Gen. 1/2**: Double-tap controls
- **AirPods Pro (1st gen)**: Adaptive EQ, Spatial audio with head tracking, ANC, Transparency, Off Mode, Pressure sensor controls
- **AirPods Max**: Adaptive EQ, Spatial audio with head tracking, ANC, Transparency, Off Mode, Uses Digital Crown control
- **AirPods Gen. 3**: Adaptive EQ, Spatial audio with head tracking, Pressure sensor controls
- **AirPods Pro 2**: Adaptive EQ, Voice Isolation, Spatial audio with head tracking, ANC, Transparency, Adaptive, Off Mode (Has to be allowed since this model), Conversational Awareness, Personalized Volume, Hearing Aid support, Touch controls
- **AirPods 4**: Adaptive EQ, Voice Isolation, Spatial audio with head tracking, Personalized Volume, Pressure sensor controls
- **AirPods 4 (ANC)**: Adaptive EQ, Voice Isolation, Spatial audio with head tracking, ANC, Transparancy, Adaptive, Off Mode (Has to be allowed), Personalized Volume, Pressure sensor controls
- **AirPods Pro 3**: Adaptive EQ, Voice Isolation, Spatial audio with head tracking, ANC, Transparency, Adaptive, Off Mode (Has to be allowed), Conversational Awareness, Personalized Volume, Hearing Aid support, Heartrate Sensor, Touch controls (Same as AirPods Pro 2 + Heartrate Sensor)
- **AirPods Max 2**: Adaptive EQ, Voice Isolation, Spatial audio with head tracking, ANC, Transparency, Adaptive, Off Mode (Has to be allowed), Conversational Awareness, Personalized Volume, reduce load noise (in place of hearing aid), Digital crown control
