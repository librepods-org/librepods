## Notes

- **AirPods Gen. 1/2**: Double-tap controls
- **AirPods Pro (1st gen)**: Adaptive EQ, Spatial audio with head tracking, ANC, Transparency, Off Mode, Pressure sensor controls
- **AirPods Max**: Adaptive EQ, Spatial audio with head tracking, ANC, Transparency, Off Mode, Uses Digital Crown control
- **AirPods Gen. 3**: Adaptive EQ, Spatial audio with head tracking, Head gestures, Pressure sensor controls
- **AirPods Pro 2**: Adaptive EQ, Voice Isolation, Spatial audio with head tracking, ANC, Transparency, Adaptive, Off Mode (Has to be allowed since this model), Conversational Awareness, Personalized Volume, Hearing Aid support, Loud Sound Reduction, Sleep detection, Head gestures, Touch controls
- **AirPods 4**: Adaptive EQ, Voice Isolation, Spatial audio with head tracking, Personalized Volume, Sleep detection, Head gestures, Pressure sensor controls
- **AirPods 4 (ANC)**: Adaptive EQ, Voice Isolation, Spatial audio with head tracking, ANC, Transparancy, Adaptive, Off Mode (Has to be allowed), Conversational Awareness, Personalized Volume, Sleep detection, Head gestures, Pressure sensor controls
- **AirPods 5**: ANC, Transparency, Adaptive, Conversational Awareness, Personalized Volume, Sleep detection, Head gestures, Configurable stem actions
- **AirPods 5 (Wireless Charging Case)**: Same as AirPods 5 + Volume swipe
- **AirPods Pro 3**: Adaptive EQ, Voice Isolation, Spatial audio with head tracking, ANC, Transparency, Adaptive, Off Mode (Has to be allowed), Conversational Awareness, Personalized Volume, Hearing Aid support, Loud Sound Reduction, Hearing protection (EN 352), Heartrate Sensor, Sleep detection, Head gestures, Touch controls (Same as AirPods Pro 2 + Heartrate Sensor + EN 352 hearing protection)
- **AirPods Max 2**: Adaptive EQ, Voice Isolation, Spatial audio with head tracking, ANC, Transparency, Adaptive, Off Mode (Has to be allowed), Conversational Awareness, Personalized Volume, Loud Sound Reduction (in place of hearing aid), Digital crown control
